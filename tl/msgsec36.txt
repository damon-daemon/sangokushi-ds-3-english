The First Emperor of Qin. Son of King Zhuangxiang, he became King of Qin at 13. At first aided by Lu Buwei, he later took power, strengthened Qin with the Legalist Li Si, and conquered Han, Zhao, Wei, Chu, Yan and Qi to unify China. He created the title "emperor," set up commanderies, standardized weights and measures and built roads. His harsh centralized rule burdened the people. Seeking immortality, he died on a tour; Qin fell just four years later.{05}{05}{05}
